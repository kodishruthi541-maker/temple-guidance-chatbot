TEMPLE GUIDANCE INFORMATION

Temple Name:
Sri Venkateswara Temple

Temple Timings:
Morning: 6:00 AM to 12:00 PM
Evening: 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM

Visitor Guidance:
Visitors should maintain cleanliness and discipline.
Visitors should follow the instructions given by temple staff.

Dress Guidance:
Visitors should wear clean and appropriate clothing.

Pooja Information:
Different poojas and sevas may be available according to the temple schedule.

Facilities:
Drinking water
Parking
Rest areas
Information counter
Prasadam counter

General Rules:
Maintain cleanliness.
Follow queues.
Do not litter.
Follow temple staff instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: What are the temple timings?
A: Morning 6:00 AM to 12:00 PM and evening 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

Q: Where can visitors get information?
